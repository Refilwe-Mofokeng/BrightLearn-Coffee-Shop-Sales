# Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## Overview
This case study analyses transactional sales data from Bright Coffee Shop to uncover actionable insights that support revenue growth and improved product performance.

The objective was to:
- Understand key revenue drivers
- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour
- Identify opportunities to optimise product uptake and demand
- Provide data-driven recommendations to support executive decision-making

---

## Approach & Methodology

The analysis was conducted in a structured, end-to-end workflow:

### 1. Project Planning
- Defined key business questions and analytical objectives
- Structured approach using visual planning tools

### 2. Data Processing (SQL)
- Cleaned and transformed raw transactional data
- Engineered new features to enhance analysis:
  - Day classification (Weekday vs Weekend)
  - Time buckets (Morning Rush, Mid-Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night)
  - Spend buckets (Low, Medium, High)
  - Revenue calculation

### 3. Data Analysis (Excel)
- Built pivot tables to explore:
  - Revenue by product category and product
  - Revenue by store location
  - Time-based trends (monthly, daily, time of day)
  - Customer spending behaviour
- Developed visualisations to highlight key patterns and trends

### 4. Insights & Presentation
- Synthesised findings into executive-level insights
- Structured recommendations aligned to business impact
- Compiled into a presentation for decision-makers

---

## Key Insights

- **Balanced Store Performance:**  
  All locations contributed approximately 33% to total revenue, indicating consistent operations across stores.

- **Strong Revenue Growth:**  
  Revenue increased significantly over time, with notable growth from January to June.

- **Demand Concentration:**  
  Sales are heavily concentrated during weekday mornings, highlighting reliance on commuter-driven demand.

- **Product Revenue Concentration:**  
  Coffee, tea, and bakery products contribute the majority of revenue (~78%), despite a wide product offering.

- **Underutilised Product Portfolio:**  
  Secondary product categories (e.g., packaged goods, flavours, branded items) contribute minimally to overall revenue (~6%).

- **Customer Behaviour Insight:**  
  Customers tend to default to familiar products, suggesting that choice complexity limits broader product exploration.

---

## Recommendations

- **Drive Off-Peak Demand:**  
  Introduce time-based targeted promotions and offerings to increase afternoon, evening, and weekend sales.

- **Simplify Customer Choice:**  
  Highlight bestsellers and recommended items to reduce decision fatigue and guide purchasing behaviour.

- **Improve Product Visibility:**  
  Reposition and promote underperforming categories to increase awareness and uptake.

- **Leverage Core Products:**  
  Use high-performing products (coffee, tea) to drive exposure to adjacent categories through pairing and positioning.

- **Introduce a Loyalty & Rewards Programme:**  
  Implement a simple rewards system (e.g., points, stamps, or discounts) to incentivise repeat purchases and influence customer behaviour over time.  
  This can be used to:
  - Encourage visits during off-peak periods  
  - Promote underperforming product categories  
  - Increase overall customer lifetime value
